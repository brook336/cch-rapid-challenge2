Functionality & Technology

Visitors can access the ThemeScheme curated guides and object descriptions via our website hosted on GitHub Pages. Because our site will be accessed by people using a variety of mobile technologies, we will use an open-source and responsive front-end web development framework like Bootstrap. Visitors can view the website before their arrival or during their visit. We will post QR codes around featured objects so that people are aware of the project and can easily participate. Printed copies will also be provided at the services desk.
