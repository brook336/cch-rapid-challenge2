for students, parents, and educators Audience

ThemeSchemes is created for students, parents, and educators looking for a structured and age-appropriate educational experience at the Museum of Anthropology. ThemeSchemes uses language and concepts consistent with national curriculum goals to make the museum experience more accessible for school-aged visitors. Elementary-aged students will likely need their parents to navigate the website for them. Visitors outside this core audience may also benefit from this experience.
