looking inside the moa for inspirationData & information

The primary content, data, and information for the ThemeSchemes at the Museum of Anthropology website will come from the Museum of Anthropology’s current exhibits and internal collection items. Items not on display for the public or considered “culturally sensitive” will not be included in the ThemeSchemes guides.
The primary data type will be images of items collected from the MoA’s online collection system. The images will have associated metadata following the Dublin Core collection scheme, and the appropriate copyright laws will be followed.
